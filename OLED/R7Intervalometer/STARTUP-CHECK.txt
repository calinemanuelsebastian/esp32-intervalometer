INTERMITTENT STARTUP / INPUT CHECK — v1.11

1. KEEP THE FIRMWARE AND PERIPHERALS THE SAME; CHANGE ONLY THE SUPPLY
Disconnect the battery/converter output from the ESP32. Power the ESP32 only
through a known-good USB cable and source. Leave the display and encoder connected
normally to the board; disconnect the camera while testing. Do several complete
USB power cycles and try the first click and rotation each time. Do not simply
add USB on top of the battery supply: that can conceal a supply problem.
If USB-only is consistently reliable, investigate the battery/converter/switch/
connector supply path before changing button wiring or software again.
If USB-only fails too, the converter alone does not explain it; continue below.

2. CHECK WITHOUT ENTERING A MENU
Home SW:1 normally means released; SW:0 means the GPIO reads pressed. Release knob
means the software is deliberately waiting for a stable release before accepting
input. A held or LOW-at-boot input must never start a camera exposure automatically.
If the display itself is frozen, the displayed SW value is stale; use Serial.
With Serial Monitor open at 115200, send p and then v. No menu access is required.
Expected released GPIO configuration: IN=1 OUT=0 PU=1 PD=0 RAW=1.
BTN down=0 stableDown=0 armed=1 busy=0 is the usual released state.
The trace reports raw A/B and accepted rotation, raw SW, accepted presses/clicks,
page/frame counters and lock state. If the heartbeat stops too, investigate a
broader stall/reset/power fault rather than the switch alone.
Keep the monitor open before reproducing a reset to capture the startup message.
A BROWNOUT report supports a low-voltage-reset diagnosis; its absence does not
exclude brief droops, display-only faults or a failure that did not trigger reset.

3. IDENTIFY THE ACTUAL POWER CIRCUIT BEFORE ALTERING IT
The older wiring guide assumes a protected one-cell battery feeding a 5 V BOOST
converter, then the ESP32 VIN/5V pin. The fitted converter and output connection
have not been confirmed. A step-down converter cannot raise a 3.7 V cell to 5 V.
A plain 3.3 V step-down also loses regulation as battery voltage falls below its
required input/headroom. Confirm the module and whether it feeds VIN or 3V3.
Measure the 3V3 rail against GND at the ESP32 during startup and screen updates;
for a VIN-powered setup also measure VIN against GND. A multimeter can miss short
dips, so a steady reading alone cannot establish transient stability. Do not
measure VIN-to-GPIO as if it were a GPIO-to-ground input voltage.
Do not disable brownout detection to hide resets. Do not select replacement
power components until the actual converter and its loading are known.

Espressif recommends a 3.3 V supply with at least 500 mA capability for the ESP32
circuit; a whole dev board plus display also needs suitable current margin.
This is a supply design guideline, not a measured current for this device.
