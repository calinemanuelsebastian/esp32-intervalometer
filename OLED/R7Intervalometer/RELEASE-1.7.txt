ESP32 PHOTOGRAPHIC INTERVALOMETER 1.7 — 2 NOVEMBER 2026

Compiled and host-tested. Not uploaded to or physically measured on the user's
device in this session. Unequal EXIF exposure durations and the reported input
freeze are not yet confirmed resolved on hardware.

TIMING REVIEW
- The GPIO assertion previously preceded the first counter read. That read's
  latency could extend the command before the reference timestamp. The reference
  now precedes assertion; GPIO timestamp readings still bracket, rather than
  directly measure, the physical edges. They are not sensor exposure timings.
- ARDUINO_ISR_ATTR expands to nothing in this stock core configuration. The
  PulseTimer callback and GPIO helper now use explicit IRAM_ATTR out-of-line
  definitions, with direct GPIO register writes on ESP32. The final ELF confirms
  PulseTimer::alarm and PulseTimer::setPins are in .iram0.text. This avoids flash
  function fetches on the critical GPIO release path.
- gptimer_get_raw_count is still in .flash.text, and the stock core has
  CONFIG_GPTIMER_ISR_CACHE_SAFE disabled. The callback performs timestamp readback
  after releasing the outputs. The complete interrupt chain is not cache-safe;
  interrupt masking/cache disabling can still defer service. No settings writes
  are allowed during an active sequence or a manual gesture.
- Alarm setup now re-reads the timer to reject a deadline already in the past.
  Failure cancels the alarm, releases both outputs and disables further triggering
  until reboot. It does not leave a held shutter waiting for a missed alarm.
- Existing stale-IRQ exclusion, completion ownership, 64-bit timing and no-catchup
  sequence behavior are retained. No guessed compensation is subtracted from EXIF.
- Diagnostics > Timing series and Serial report count, missing readings, minimum,
  maximum and spread, plus maximum captured IRQ-entry lateness in Serial. New
  sequences reset aggregates. Dry-run results are explicitly marked DRY.

SUBSECOND / CAMERA MODES
- Bulb sec + Bulb ms allow electrical hold durations from 0.100 s to 24 h. The
  fractional field adds 0-999 ms; the minimum and maximum totals are normalized.
  A camera may reject or quantize short Bulb commands; support is not assumed.
- Shot mode = Camera sends a 50-1000 ms remote press (default 100 ms). Set the
  exposure time on the camera in M/Tv or its equivalent. The jack cannot transfer
  a 1/8000 s shutter-speed setting. This mode can also let the camera time 10 s.
- Bulb duration fields are ignored in Camera mode. The UI counts triggers, without
  capture acknowledgement. Use single-shot drive and allow enough Gap for exposure
  and saving; gap starts at remote release, not camera completion.
- A separate Capture NVS record preserves old whole-second settings. Reverting to
  older firmware cannot preserve a subsecond-only exposure configuration.

MANUAL SHUTTER / INPUTS
- Manual press debounce is 5 ms; ordinary menus retain 30 ms and the existing
  two-second hold behavior. Release is processed at the first observed open contact.
- Full-frame LCD writes are split into eight-row strips, servicing the manual
  shooting selection between transfers. Events occurring during drawing keep the
  dirty flag so a subsequent frame can show the changed state.
- Selecting Hold to shoot now asserts the focus/half-press contact (LIVE only),
  keeping the camera ready before shutter press. It remains held after shutter
  release until Back is selected or the page is exited. Autofocus/self-timer and
  camera shutter lag remain dependent on camera settings.
- Serial v enables nonblocking raw input/event/heartbeat logging, including lock
  state; p reads GPIO27 without needing the encoder. ! stops the sequence, releases
  outputs, clears lock, reapplies pull-ups and resets button/rotation state. A
  held button must be released before acting again. Recovery cannot service a
  completely stalled CPU or fix an undiagnosed power fault.

DISPLAY
- Diagnostics > LCD colors previews Native, Swap R/B, Invert and Swap + invert
  against labeled R/G/B/C/M/Y/white/black samples. Click saves; hold cancels.
- Initial Swap R/B calibration addresses the reported cyan->yellow symptom and
  is stored separately from Theme. No panel offset/rotation change is imposed.
- Cyan's white body text and yellow Dry run warning remain intentional accents.

INSTALL / VALIDATION
Replace the entire R7Intervalometer sketch folder. New files Capture.h, Panel.h
and PulseTimer.cpp must accompany App.cpp and the other headers. Open the .ino;
Arduino automatically compiles both .cpp files. See GHID-1.7-RO.txt and README.md.

Ten additional scenarios cover fractional commands, Camera mode, record migration
and persistence, five-ms manual response, press/release within LCD transfers,
color mapping/preview/cancel/save, Serial recovery and overdue alarm setup.
