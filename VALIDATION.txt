Shared core comparison: PASS (eight identical files).
All packaged top-level C++/header files match the source copied into the successful builds.
Sequence, Controls, Capture, Exposure and TimerPlatform remain unchanged from v1.11.
PulseTimer and Input changed together in both editions; App/display code is edition-specific.
Bounded setup retries keep shutter LOW, reject a persistent 2 ms setup stall, and accept a later timely attempt.
Missing callback: outputs released, sequence stopped, zero frames counted, timer fault latched.
A missing callback during a screen transfer yields the frame for the foreground deadline check.
Settings writes and menu actions no longer execute inside the frame transfer.
Pending rotation remains queued until the main loop; continuous turns cannot starve complete frames.
TFT framebuffer-allocation fault originally hung the test; now loop/Serial remain usable and capture is blocked.
OLED allocation failure remains guarded; transient I2C recovery tests also pass.
Normal modeled GPIO clicks -> three-frame sequence succeeds in LIVE and DRY modes on each edition.
Driver mock asserts no GPTimer get-count/set-alarm call occurs under the application mux.
IRAM audit follows reachable instruction boundaries, avoiding false calls from Xtensa alignment padding.
It checks normal direct call paths and the pinned indirect sites, not worst-case latency or panic paths.
The final assertion clock check/critical section is in IRAM as well as the release callback.
No proven driver lock-order deadlock is claimed; the patch removes unnecessary driver work from the app lock.
No hardware flashing, power measurements or camera/connector timing measurements performed.
The foreground deadline fallback cannot run during a total CPU, driver or power lockup.
The intermittent physical freeze and EXIF variation are NOT confirmed resolved.
SSD1306 128x64 remains the assumed OLED identification.
