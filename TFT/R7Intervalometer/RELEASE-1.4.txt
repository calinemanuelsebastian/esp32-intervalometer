R7 INTERVALOMETER 1.4 — 9 OCTOBER 2026

Full firmware release package. Software has been compiled and tested on the host;
it has not been flashed or physically validated on the assembled device here.

CHANGES FROM 1.3
- Manual Shutter carousel entry: debounced press asserts focus + shutter; release
  ends the output on the first observed open contact. A long hold remains a manual
  exposure rather than invoking the two-second menu Back/Stop behavior.
- Entering Manual Shutter does not trigger. Back is a separate selection, chosen
  after releasing the button. Rotation cannot change selection during a gesture.
- Dry run applies to manual and timed operation. Existing exposure settings and
  saved Dry run state are preserved. No automatic shot at startup.
- Default menu sensitivity is two decoded encoder detents per item, configurable
  to 1–4 using Settings > Menu clicks. Its data is stored separately from the
  existing exposure configuration, preserving prior saved settings.
- Numeric editing is one unit per detent; speed-based acceleration is removed.
- Manual display shows commanded hold duration. Backlight goes off at shot start,
  wakes on rotation, times out during the hold, and returns on release.
- Verbose input logging is OFF by default; on-device diagnostics and serial
  diagnostics commands remain available. Output transitions still log best-effort.
- All v1.3 fixes are retained. Sequence.h is unchanged from the reviewed v1.3 engine.

LIMITS
Manual focus and shutter are asserted together as a remote full press; the timed
sequence's 500ms focus lead is not inserted into a manually held exposure.
The initial press is debounced for 30ms. Output release is serviced in the main
loop; display/library calls can add latency, and a stopped processor cannot close
the shutter until it runs or loses power. Actual camera behavior depends on camera
mode, drive mode, focus and Bulb timer settings. The cable supplies no feedback.

The reported problem requiring a physical battery disconnect has not been
reproduced or resolved by these changes. See POWER-AND-ENCLOSURE.txt for switch
placement and the distinction between load OFF and a battery master disconnect.
